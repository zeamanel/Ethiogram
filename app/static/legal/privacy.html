Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

Ethiogram ("we", "us") helps businesses run AI assistants on Telegram. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — your Telegram ID, name, username, and chosen language, provided by Telegram when you open the bot.
  • Business data — if you run a business on Ethiogram: your business name, catalog, storefront settings, and configuration.
  • Messages — conversations with a business bot are processed to generate replies and may be retained by that business to improve its assistant.
  • Usage — basic logs (timestamps, message counts, billing events) needed to operate and bill the service.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver bot replies and run the features you use.
  • To operate your storefront, dashboard, and billing.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse.

3. AI processing

Messages may be sent to third-party AI model providers to generate responses. We send only what is needed to answer; we do not sell your messages.

4. Sharing

When you message a business, that business can see your conversation. We share data with service providers (hosting, AI, payments) only to run Ethiogram, and when required by law.

5. Retention

We keep account and business data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your data, subject to legal and billing obligations.

6. Your choices

  • Stop a bot at any time with /stop or by blocking it.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, your data via support.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact @ethiogram_support.
