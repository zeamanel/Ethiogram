Our Story
Fashion rooted in Ethiopian craft

Selam Store began in Bole with a simple belief: traditional Ethiopian craftsmanship deserves a modern home. We work directly with local weavers, leatherworkers, and silversmiths across Addis Ababa.

Every Habesha Kemis is handwoven. Every bag is cut and stitched by hand. When you order from Selam Store, you're supporting Ethiopian artisans — and getting a piece made to last.

Chat with us on Telegram any time. Our assistant answers instantly, in Amharic or English, and helps you find exactly what you're looking for.

Frequently asked
Do you deliver across Addis Ababa? +
Yes — we deliver anywhere in Addis Ababa. Delivery is free on orders over 500 ETB, and a small flat fee applies below that. Most orders arrive within 1–2 days.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Telebirr, CBE Birr, and card payments through Chapa. You can pay securely right inside our Telegram chat when you place your order.
Are the Habesha Kemis handwoven? +
Every Habesha Kemis we sell is handwoven by local artisans in Addis Ababa, with traditional tilet embroidery. No machine-made copies.
Can I order in Amharic? +
Absolutely. Our Telegram assistant replies in both Amharic and English, automatically — just message us in whichever you prefer.
